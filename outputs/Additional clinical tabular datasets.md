# Sixteen prepared tables unlock statistical power

**Run the sixteen already-bundled local tasks before downloading anything new.** The complexity-versus-gap analysis currently has **20 completed points** (`scripts/analyse_uci_complexity_gaps.py` PAIRS); the repo already holds **36 `dataset.yaml` bundles**, so **16 prepared tasks are free extra points** and are the cheapest way to raise *n* for a dataset-level Spearman or logistic fit. After those locals are in the PAIRS list, the remaining UCI/OpenML well of named patient-level classification is largely depleted: living tabular suites (OpenML-CC18, TabArena-v0.1, AMLB, TabZilla, PMLB) mostly recycle Cleveland, WDBC, ILPD, Diabetes130US, maternal risk, and thyroid already in this corpus ([OpenML-CC18 Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.03731v3); [TabArena Table B.1](https://openreview.net/attachment?id=jZqCqpCLdU&name=supplementary_material)). New points should buy **empty cells of complexity space**—missingness, imbalance, *p*/*n*, and specialty—not another heart or breast clone. The highest-value **new** public downloads are Regensburg pediatric appendicitis, PhysioNet Challenge 2012 ICU mortality, Challenge 2019 early sepsis, NHANES measured hypertension or CKD, METABRIC PAM50/3-gene subtype, Mexico’s COVID line-list, and Mendeley postpartum depression. Registration (NFHS-5, SHHS) and credentialed PhysioNet (HiRID, MIMIC-IV-ED) are later expansion, not the next sprint. Tabular-LLM papers themselves treat **dataset as the independent unit** and warn that five-to-ten famous UCI tables overstate generality ([TabZilla](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.02997); [MedBench](https://odr.chalmers.se/server/api/core/bitstreams/7a00790a-af3e-4379-bbc1-96a068482e74/content)).

## Sixteen local bundles raise n from 20 to 36 without a download

The analysis unit is **one dataset/task = one point**. Completed PAIRS cover Cleveland heart, HCV category, mammographic mass, heart-failure mortality, ILPD, Diabetes130 30-day readmission, cervical biopsy, WiDS 2021 diabetes, Coimbra breast, hepatitis survival, bone-marrow transplant death, Haberman, maternal risk, cirrhosis death, SUPPORT2 hospital death, AIDS CID event, glioma GBM, vertebral pathology, Ljubljana breast recurrence, and CDC diabetes indicators. That mix is already heavier on cardiology, hepatology, endocrinology, and breast than on dermatology, thyroid, arrhythmia, Parkinson voice, MI complications, fetal monitoring, thoracic surgery, or autism screening. The **16 prepared but unanalyzed bundles** sit on disk as `dataset.yaml` plus pipeline-ready tables; folding them into PAIRS and rerunning nested 5-fold CV (Qwen3-4B zero-shot, XGBoost) is strictly cheaper than any external ingest.

These sixteen do **not** all expand complexity space equally: WDBC and WBC original sit next to two breast tasks already in the 20-point set, and `uci_thyroid` / `uci_thyroid_recur` are one specialty. They still add independent *rows* to the gap regression, which is the binding constraint at *n*=20. TabZilla’s complaint that nearly all prior tabular studies use **fewer than 50 datasets**, and Grinsztajn’s working size of **45**, imply that 20→36 is the single largest design improvement available this week ([TabZilla](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.02997)). Do not skip this tier to chase a new UCI ID.

## Immediate public tables that occupy empty complexity cells

After the locals, **do not scrape OpenML-CC18 or TabArena for “unused medical” rows.** Confirmed leftovers in those suites are Pima diabetes (OpenML 37 / TabArena 46921), blood-transfusion RFM (OpenML 1464 / TabArena 46913), OpenML 38 `sick` (authors: a merged hypothyroid), MiceProtein (mouse proteomics), and `hiva_agnostic` (3,845 × 1,618 anonymous categoricals) ([OpenML-CC18](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.03731v3); [TabArena](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16791v1)). None of those is the next independent clinical point. The useful remainder lives **outside** the living AutoML suites: recent UCI Health and Medicine deposits, PhysioNet open challenges, CDC XPTs, cBioPortal clinical TSVs, Dryad TRIPOD CSVs, and one post-cutoff Mendeley table used by LLMTabBench.

Pipeline constraints that filter every row below: public tabular clinical **classification**, **2–7 classes**, min class count **10**, named predictors renderable as English text, **no Kaggle login preferred**, no follow-up-time leakage, large tables subsampled to **2–5k**, evaluated with the existing 5-fold nested CV. Rank is fitness for a **new complexity region**, not download popularity.

The Framingham **teaching** extract (**4,434** persons, **39** file variables, three exams) is readable CHD risk-factor names and is **not** Cleveland, but BioLINCC states teaching datasets are **unsuitable for publication as scientific findings about Framingham**; the same anonymized table ships in CRAN `riskCommunicator` ([BioLINCC teaching](https://biolincc.nhlbi.nih.gov/teaching/); [framingham.R](https://rdrr.io/cran/riskCommunicator/src/R/framingham.R)). Treat Pima as a contamination control, not a new clinical regime.

Dryad sepsis surveillance (3,862 encounters, XLSX) is a maybe if the alert definition is not circular in the same vitals ([doi:10.5061/dryad.vh380vb](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.vh380vb)). Challenge 2023’s clinical companion (*n*=1,020, *p*≈7, CPC good/poor) is too thin to occupy a new ICU-lab cell and the official task is EEG/ECG.

A practical next download wave, once the 16 locals are running, is **Regensburg + Challenge 2012 + NHANES HTN/CKD + METABRIC subtype + postpartum** (five points, five specialties, no login). Challenge 2019 is the sixth if aggregation time is acceptable. That package moves *n* from 36 toward ~41–42 and actually changes the support of missingness, *p*/*n*, and specialty rather than cloning UCI heart.

## Registration and credentialed EHR buy hospital-scale missingness later

**Simple registration (tier C)** is justified when a table is patient-level, named, and geographically or physiologically unlike the 36, and a click-through is shorter than a CITI course. **India NFHS-5** (DHS 2019–21: **724,115** women, **636,669** households) is the cleanest such add: register, wait **24–48 hours**, subsample 2–5k, and build a 2-class anemia or hypertension table from biomarker constructs — a non-US survey instrument, not another BRFSS clone ([DHS survey 541](https://www.dhsprogram.com/methodology/survey/survey-display-541.cfm); [access instructions](https://dhsprogram.com/data/Access-Instructions.cfm)). **SHHS Visit 1** on NSRR (**5,804** shared participants; documentation public, full CSV behind a Data Access and Use Agreement) uniquely fills **sleep medicine + CVD**; composition includes ARIC, CHS, and Framingham Offspring, so it is not a second independent Framingham point ([SHHS introduction](https://sleepdata.org/datasets/shhs/pages/04-dataset-introduction.md)). SEER Research Data requires per-user registration and **SEER*Stat**, not a public CSV, and Research Plus is tighter still ([SEER access](https://seer.cancer.gov/data/access.html)); skip SEER for this sprint. WiDS 2020 GOSSIS ICU death (**91,713** labeled encounters, first 24 h, PhysioNet Restricted DUA **or** Kaggle) is already-flat but is the **same GOSSIS family** as in-corpus WiDS 2021 diabetes and is therefore **not an independent complexity region** ([WiDS 2020](https://physionet.org/content/widsdatathon2020/1.0.0/)). Project Data Sphere originals behind MediTab are registration-gated; the authors’ **CTGAN synthetic** NCT tables (53–3,871 patients, event rates 0.02–0.94) are on GitHub if a *synthetic trial-schema* point is wanted, with the caveat that CTGAN is not EHR ([MediTab Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.12081v4); [Project Data Sphere](https://data.projectdatasphere.org/projectdatasphere/html/access)). HINT/TOP trial-outcome phases are **trial-level** success/failure (*n*=1,787 / 6,102 / 4,576, 6 features), a different observational unit than a patient row — out of scope unless the project explicitly changes the analysis unit.

**Credentialed PhysioNet/EHR (tier D)** is the only reliable path off the UCI island for **hospital-scale missingness**, which is exactly where frozen LLMs have already been shown to struggle: CL4Health found zero-shot Llama-2/Meditron weak on templated MIMIC-IV mortality ([CL4Health](https://aclanthology.org/2024.cl4health-1.23.pdf)). Access is consistently CITI “Data or Specimens Only Research” plus a PhysioNet DUA except AmsterdamUMCdb, which also wants a practicing-intensivist reference ([Sauer et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9150442/)). Rank by how close the deposit is to **one named `patients.csv`**, not by ICU brand prestige.

There is **no** commonly redistributed public MIMIC mortality CSV that bypasses the DUA; FIDDLE inherits MIMIC/eICU credentialing. “Publicly available ICU database” in the 2025 review means downloadable **after** credentialing, not UCI-style anonymous CSV.

## Heart, breast, thyroid, and leaked tables add no independent points

Reject anything that is already one of the **36 bundles**, a family duplicate, below the sample floor, non-clinical, image/waveform/genomic-primary, anonymous high-*p*, or a follow-up-leaked survival table dressed as baseline classification.

**Already in the 36 (never re-download):** Cleveland heart, CKD, dermatology, HCV blood-donor, mammographic mass, WDBC, thyroid/hypothyroid, arrhythmia, heart failure, ILPD, Diabetes130, cervical risk, WiDS 2021, Coimbra breast, early diabetes, SPECTF, hepatitis, obesity, bone marrow, Oxford Parkinsons, Haberman, MI lethal, maternal risk, thyroid recurrence, cirrhosis, SUPPORT2, AIDS CID, glioma, retinopathy Debrecen, cardiotocography, thoracic, WBC original, vertebral, adult autism, Ljubljana breast recurrence, CDC diabetes.

**Family duplicates:** Statlog Heart (UCI: “similar to a database already present”); Hungarian/VA/Switzerland heart; SPECT 95 (same **267** patients as SPECTF, features F1–F22 labeled “partial diagnosis”); WPBC vs WDBC/WBC; autism children/adolescent vs adult autism 426; Parkinson speech UCI 470 (**756×754** DSP, 3 rows/person, `.rar`) and telemonitoring UPDRS vs Oxford 174; OpenML 38 `sick` vs `uci_thyroid` (CC18 authors: merged hypothyroid); TabZilla heart-c/heart-h vs Cleveland; OpenML/TabArena copies of ILPD, wdbc, breast-w, Diabetes130US, maternal_health_risk; KEEL’s entire medical chest (Cleveland, hepatitis, wisconsin, Ljubljana, SPECTF, Haberman); **BRFSS 2023** hypertension/stroke/depression (same survey family as UCI 891 CDC diabetes); **WiDS 2020** GOSSIS mortality sibling of WiDS 2021; **TCGA-LGG/GBM** clinical vs in-corpus glioma; **Risk Factor CKD** 200×28 until proven independent of UCI 336.

**Not another heart/breast clone, even if unused:** Kaggle `fedesoriano/heart-failure-prediction` (**918×11**, four-hospital CAD) is TabLLM’s “Heart” and is distinct from Cleveland 303 and Chicco 299, but it needs a **Kaggle login**, is a popular web table (medium contamination), and is still CAD — the opposite of the diversity goal ([TabLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.10723); [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fedesoriano/heart-failure-prediction)). KEEL/ESL **saheart** (**462×9**, Western Cape men) is the same story without Kaggle. Blood transfusion UCI 176 / OpenML 1464 is **Business RFM**, donor return, not a clinical endpoint ([UCI 176](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/176/blood+transfusion+service+center)). BUPA liver disorders historically encodes drinks, not liver disease.

**Too small or unreadable:** Lung cancer 32×56; fertility 100×10; cervical behavior 72×19; caesarian 80; immunotherapy 90; post-operative 90; acute inflammations **120×6** (Zhu et al.: **1.00 accuracy for every model** — ceiling plus web-famous) ([PMC13274287](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13274287/)); early PD speech 130; autism adolescent 104; breast tissue 106; KEEL appendicitis **106×7 named At1–At7**; lymphography 148 *and* restricted; Veterans lung **137**; MIMIC-IV demo **100**; OSMI workplace survey n=51.

**Wrong modality or unit:** AIM ’94 Diabetes UCI 34 (time-series insulin log, not Pima); Parkinsons telemonitoring (UPDRS regression); infrared thermography (oral temperature); NHANES age-prediction subset; healthcare_insurance_expenses; TCGA Kidney **1.02k × 60.66k** transcripts; Arcene mass-spec; DARWIN handwriting; Challenges **2020/2021** (12-lead ECG), **2022** (phonocardiograms); EEG/EMG/IMU HAR; WHO GHO **indicators**, not patients ([GHO FAQ](https://www.who.int/data/gho/info/frequently-asked-questions)); UK Biobank (~500k, restricted cloud); German Statlog **credit**; call-center satisfaction; MoleculeNet BBBP; HINT/TOP unless the unit of analysis changes; MediTab NCT *raw* without CTGAN if PDS login is out of scope.

**Class-count failures without heroic collapsing:** primary tumor (TabZilla: **21** classes); audiology (**24**); arrhythmia already bundled (16 groups — only keep if the local bundle already collapsed to 2–7); MSK-IMPACT 2017 (**10,000** cases) unless OncoTree is collapsed to ≤7 sites ([MSK catalog 10438](https://datacatalog.mskcc.org/dataset/10438)); METABRIC INTCLUST (10).

**Contamination policy for any table that survives:** Silvestri et al. show LLMs can hold **latent knowledge of public tables when column names are preserved**; LLMTabBench therefore ships a post-Oct-2024 suite whose healthcare member is postpartum ([arXiv:2510.20351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20351) via [LLMTabBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24417v2)). If the scientific claim is “Qwen3-4B zero-shot uses clinical priors,” Pima/Cleveland/WDBC cannot support it (the last two are already in-corpus). If the claim is a **dataset-level gap regression**, famous UCI sets remain valid **as long as a web-famous indicator is a covariate**. New points should preferentially be post-cutoff (postpartum), assembled XPTs (NHANES), PhysioNet-open (2012/2019), or credentialed EHR (HiRID, MIMIC-IV-ED) — not another CC18 medical chestnut.

## Conclusion

The binding constraint is **number and diversity of dataset-level points**, not the existence of more UCI Health and Medicine cards. Folding the **16 prepared bundles** into PAIRS is the only intervention that raises *n* without ingest risk; the subsequent public wave should be **Regensburg, Challenge 2012, NHANES measured HTN or CKD, METABRIC subtype, and postpartum**, with Challenge 2019 if a collapse script is cheap. That sequence fills pediatric surgery, ICU missingness, survey+labs, molecular oncology, and categorical psychiatry — cells the current cardiology/hepatology/endocrinology/breast mix does not occupy — while respecting 2–7 classes, named English predictors, and no follow-up-time leakage. Credentialed HiRID and MIMIC-IV-ED remain the correct later instruments for European high-frequency ICU and ED triage; they are not substitutes for running what is already on disk.
