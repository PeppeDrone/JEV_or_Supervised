Figure 1. Study overview of the clinical prediction benchmark.

Forty-five binary and multiclass clinical classification tasks are compared using the same declared predictors. For ten large tables XGBoost uses the whole table and System 1 reads a capped random subsample. XGBoost receives tabular predictors and is trained within stratified five-fold outer cross-validation, with three-fold inner tuning. For System 1, the predictors are rendered deterministically as patient text and read by frozen Qwen3-4B through AnyJev L0, without training labels. Per-task balanced accuracy is calculated from pooled out-of-fold predictions, with the frozen language model applied directly, without training. Differences are reported relative to XGBoost; bootstrap metric intervals are patient resamples conditional on the fitted models. Across tasks, performance differences are examined in relation to task complexity and to the explanation and detail supplied by the dataset specification used to render the patient text. The illustrations are conceptual symbols rather than observed data or model architecture diagrams.
