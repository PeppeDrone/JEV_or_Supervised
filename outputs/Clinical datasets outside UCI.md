# Eight tables occupy empty complexity cells

**The next independent statistical points for this project’s dataset-level complexity versus XGBoost-minus-LLM balanced-accuracy analysis are eight no-login, non-UCI tables: PhysioNet Challenge 2012 ICU mortality, Challenge 2019 early sepsis, NHANES 2021–2023 measured hypertension, METABRIC PAM50 or 3-gene subtype, Mendeley postpartum depression, Mexico’s SSA COVID line-list, Dryad northern-Italy ER blood-cell differentials, and the Zenodo hip-fracture extended-LOS TRIPOD+AI deposit.** Those eight occupy ICU named-lab missingness, extreme irregular-lab missingness, survey-plus-labs, molecular oncology, perinatal psychiatry, infectious-disease coded missingness, emergency hematology, and orthopedic trauma — cells the current cardiology/hepatology/endocrinology/breast mix does not cover. Living AutoML suites (OpenML-CC18, TabArena, AMLB, TabZilla, PMLB) contribute no unused named patient-level clinical table that is free of UCI origin; their medical leftovers are Pima, blood-transfusion RFM, merged hypothyroid, mouse proteomics, and anonymous HIV flags ([OpenML-CC18 Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.03731v3); [TabArena](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.16791v1)). Tabular-LLM papers treat **dataset as the independent unit** and warn that five-to-ten famous teaching tables overstate generality ([TabZilla](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.02997); [MedBench](https://odr.chalmers.se/server/api/core/bitstreams/7a00790a-af3e-4379-bbc1-96a068482e74/content)). The sixteen already-prepared local bundles are UCI-origin and out of scope here. Rank below is fitness for a **new complexity region**, not download popularity: 2–7 classes, min class ≥10, named English-renderable predictors, subsample 2–5k, no follow-up-time leakage.

## Eight no-login tables occupy empty complexity cells

The analysis unit remains **one dataset/task = one point**. Challenge 2012 is the strongest ICU add because Outcomes-a/b/c are now all posted on `physionet-open`, the 48-hour cut is specified, and the label is in-hospital death rather than the in-corpus WiDS 2021 *diabetes* label ([PhysioNet Challenge 2012](https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2012/1.0.0/)). Challenge 2019 is the strongest public missingness/imbalance add: 40 named hourly columns, 40,336 subjects, and a low-prevalence deterioration label after a fixed *t*₀ collapse ([challenge-2019/1.0.0](https://www.physionet.org/content/challenge-2019/1.0.0/)). NHANES measured hypertension is the cleanest public-health add relative to in-corpus BRFSS diabetes (UCI 891): NCHS Data Brief 511 used **n=6,084** examined adults 18+ and found **47.7%** hypertension (SBP ≥130 or DBP ≥80 or medication), so both classes far exceed 10 ([NCHS Data Brief 511](https://cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db511.htm); [NHANES 2021–2023 DataPage](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/search/DataPage.aspx?Cycle=2021-2023)). METABRIC is the only no-login oncology TSV with a native 2–7 class clinical label: **2,509** patients, ~15 usable clinical columns after dropping survival, PAM50+claudin-low up to 7 labels or 3-gene 4 classes, claudin-low **218 / 2,509 (8.7%)** ([MSK catalog 11457](https://datacatalog.mskcc.org/dataset/11457); [cBioPortal brca_metabric](https://github.com/cBioPortal/datahub/tree/master/public/brca_metabric); [PMC10216296](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10216296/)). Postpartum depression is LLMTabBench’s post-October-2024 healthcare member — **800 × 44** (1 numeric / 43 categorical), **43.8%** positive, CC BY 4.0 — and is the lowest-contamination public psychiatry table in this literature ([LLMTabBench Table XIV](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24417v2); [Mendeley 4nznnrk8cg v2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/4nznnrk8cg/2)). Mexico’s SSA line-list is the only national COVID table that is patient-level, comorbidity-named, and click-to-download; filter `CLASIFICACION_FINAL` 1–3 and subsample 2–5k ([gob.mx datos abiertos](https://www.gob.mx/salud/documentos/datos-abiertos-152127)). The Dryad ER complete-blood-count table is the highest-yield 2020–2026 paper-supplement find: **11,052** consecutive adult ER patients, death **1,597 / 9,455** or ward admission **4,563 / 6,489**, AUROC 0.74–0.79 so not a ceiling table ([Dryad 10.5061/dryad.dncjsxm5g](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.dncjsxm5g)). The Zenodo hip-fracture deposit is an explicit TRIPOD+AI leakage audit with development **n=1,137** and 43 candidate predictors timed to prediction ([Zenodo 22866907](https://zenodo.org/records/22866907)).

## Registration and credentialed EHR buy hospital-scale missingness

**Tier B (simple registration, not IRB)** is justified when a table is patient-level, named, and geographically or physiologically unlike the eight, and a click-through is shorter than a CITI course. **India NFHS-5** is the cleanest such add: DHS 2019–21, **724,115** women and **636,669** households, biomarkers including anemia, anthropometry, **measured blood pressure**, and blood glucose/HbA1c; register, wait **24–48 hours**, subsample 2–5k, and build a 2-class anemia or measured-hypertension table — a non-US survey instrument, not another BRFSS clone ([DHS survey 541](https://www.dhsprogram.com/methodology/survey/survey-display-541.cfm); [access instructions](https://dhsprogram.com/data/Access-Instructions.cfm)). **SHHS Visit 1** on NSRR (**5,804** shared participants) uniquely fills **sleep medicine + CVD**; documentation is public, full CSV sits behind a Data Access and Use Agreement; composition includes ARIC, CHS, and Framingham Offspring, so it is not a second independent Framingham point ([SHHS introduction](https://sleepdata.org/datasets/shhs/pages/04-dataset-introduction.md)). Framingham teaching extract (**4,434** persons, **39** file variables) is readable CHD risk-factor names and is **not** Cleveland, but BioLINCC states teaching datasets are **unsuitable for publication as scientific findings about Framingham**; use exam-1 covariates only, drop `TIME`/`TIMECVD`, and drop `PREVCHD` if *Y* is incident CHD ([BioLINCC teaching](https://biolincc.nhlbi.nih.gov/teaching/); [riskCommunicator framingham](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/riskCommunicator/html/framingham.html)). AACR GENIE 20.0-public (**>242,000** patients) has a true clinical TSV, but public columns are sparse (SEX, race, ethnicity, center, ONCOTREE_CODE, SAMPLE_TYPE, CANCER_TYPE) and access is **account + terms**, with Synapse now a two-step US-regulation request ([aacrprojectgenie.org/data](https://aacrprojectgenie.org/data/); [genie.synapse.org/Access](https://genie.synapse.org/Access)). SEER Research Data requires per-user registration and **SEER*Stat**, not a public CSV — skip for this sprint ([SEER access](https://seer.cancer.gov/data/access.html)). MediTab’s **CTGAN synthetic** NCT tables (53–3,871 patients, event rates 0.02–0.94) sit on GitHub if a *synthetic trial-schema* point is wanted; raw Project Data Sphere is registration-gated and the rows are not EHR ([MediTab Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.12081v4); [MediTab GitHub](https://github.com/RyanWangZf/MediTab)). HINT/TOP trial-outcome phases are **trial-level** success/failure (*n*=1,787 / 6,102 / 4,576, 6 features) — a different observational unit, out of scope unless the project changes the analysis unit.

**Tier C (credentialed PhysioNet/EHR)** is the only reliable path to **hospital-scale missingness**, which is exactly where frozen LLMs have already been shown to struggle: CL4Health found zero-shot Llama-2/Meditron weak on templated MIMIC-IV mortality ([CL4Health](https://aclanthology.org/2024.cl4health-1.23.pdf)). Access is consistently CITI “Data or Specimens Only Research” plus a PhysioNet DUA except AmsterdamUMCdb, which also wants a practicing-intensivist reference ([Sauer et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9150442/)). Rank by how close the deposit is to **one named `patients.csv`**. Pick **at most one** public ICU-death table (Challenge 2012) and **at most one** credentialed MIMIC-era mortality extract later; 2012 + Harutyunyan + YAIB MIMIC-IV mortality + FIDDLE MIMIC mortality are one physiology family.

## UCI mirrors, GOSSIS siblings, and CAD clones add no independent points

**Tier D** rejects anything that is a UCI ID or UCI mirror, a family duplicate of an in-corpus table, below the sample floor, non-clinical, image/waveform/genomic-primary, anonymous high-*p*, a follow-up-leaked survival table dressed as baseline classification, or a second copy of a complexity cell already filled above.

**UCI and UCI-origin (hard reject, including GitHub/Zenodo/OpenML/KEEL/Mendeley copies):** Regensburg pediatric appendicitis (UCI 938 / Zenodo 7669215), Sepsis Survival Minimal Clinical Records (UCI 827), HCC Survival (UCI 423), Egyptian HCV (UCI 503), Exasens (UCI 523/566), NPHA (UCI 936), SPECT 95 (same 267 patients as in-corpus SPECTF), WPBC, Pima OpenML 37 (withdrawn from UCI over consent; zeros encode missingness; 2-hour glucose entangled with the WHO label; memorization magnet — [Pima notice](https://anerlang.org/reference/datasets/licenses/OPENML-PIMA-NOTICE.txt)), OpenML 38 `sick` (CC18 authors: merged hypothyroid), OpenML/TabArena copies of ILPD, wdbc, breast-w, Diabetes130US, maternal_health_risk, blood-transfusion UCI 176, and KEEL’s entire medical chest (Cleveland, hepatitis, wisconsin, Ljubljana, SPECTF, Haberman, saheart). Mendeley “heart attack” `yrwd336rkz`, breast `dbz42w9x8h`, and Pima-style diabetes `nxnty5g7y6` match canonical UCI column names and are mirrors even when the landing page claims a local hospital. Kaggle `fedesoriano/heart-failure-prediction` (**918×11**) is TabLLM’s “Heart” and is a **merge of UCI Heart Disease sources** (Cleveland, Hungarian, Switzerland, Long Beach VA, Statlog) — CAD clone plus Kaggle login ([TabLLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.10723); [rikhuijzer/heart-disease-dataset](https://github.com/rikhuijzer/heart-disease-dataset)).

**Family duplicates of tables already in this corpus:** WiDS 2020 GOSSIS ICU death (**91,713** labeled encounters; [widsdatathon2020](https://physionet.org/content/widsdatathon2020/1.0.0/)) is the **same GOSSIS family** as in-corpus WiDS 2021 diabetes and is therefore not an independent complexity region. BRFSS 2023 hypertension/stroke/depression (n=433,323; [BRFSS 2023](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/annual_data/annual_2023.html)) is the same survey architecture as UCI 891 CDC diabetes. TCGA-LGG/GBM clinical overlaps in-corpus glioma. METABRIC OS plus METABRIC subtype plus GBSG plus TCGA-BRCA clinical would be four breast points, not four independent cells.

**Wrong access, wrong unit, or too small:** MIMIC-IV and MIMIC-IV-ED demos and TinyEHR (**n=100**). Hugging Face unofficial MIMIC dumps. Electric Sheep Africa stroke 10k tables (synthetic unless a primary hospital paper is attached). WHO GHO (indicators, not patients; [GHO FAQ](https://www.who.int/data/gho/info/frequently-asked-questions)). UK Biobank (~500k, restricted cloud). France SNDS, Japan NDB microdata, Korea HIRA sample, NHS HES, CIHI DAD, AIHW unit-record, Nordic registries. Call-center satisfaction, MoleculeNet BBBP, blood-transfusion donor-return RFM, BUPA drinks-not-liver-disease. Challenges 2020/2021 (12-lead ECG), 2022 (phonocardiograms); Challenge 2023 clinical companion (*n*=1,020, *p*≈7, CPC) is too thin to occupy a new ICU-lab cell. PRAIM mammography, COPD cough/breath features, Stabl cell-free-RNA preeclampsia (*n*_patients=48), PeerJ preeclampsia-in-COVID n=100, PLOS liver-disease COVID n=80, KEEL appendicitis **106×7** named At1–At7, Acute Inflammations n=120 (Zhu et al.: **1.00 accuracy for every model** — and it is UCI 184). Giesa POD Dryad deposits are feature-statistics, not patients. Dialysis-session hypotension is session-level, not IID patients.

**Contamination policy:** Silvestri et al. show LLMs can hold **latent knowledge of public tables when column names are preserved** ([arXiv:2510.20351](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20351) via [LLMTabBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.24417v2)). If the scientific claim is “Qwen3-4B zero-shot uses clinical priors,” Pima/Cleveland/WDBC cannot support it. If the claim is a **dataset-level gap regression**, famous tables remain valid **as long as a web-famous indicator is a covariate**. New points should preferentially be post-cutoff (postpartum), assembled XPTs (NHANES), PhysioNet-open (2012/2019), ministry line-lists (Mexico), Dryad/Zenodo TRIPOD CSVs (ER-CBC, hip-fracture), or credentialed EHR (HiRID, MIMIC-IV-ED) — not another CC18 medical chestnut.

## Conclusion

The UCI/OpenML well of named patient-level clinical classification is depleted **and** out of scope; the remaining worldwide supply that actually moves a dataset-level Spearman or logistic fit is a short list of PhysioNet-open challenges, NCHS XPTs, one cBioPortal subtype TSV, one post-cutoff Mendeley psychiatry table, one Mexican line-list, and a handful of Dryad/Zenodo hospital CSVs. Ingesting Challenge 2012, NHANES measured hypertension, METABRIC subtype, postpartum, Mexico COVID, and ER-CBC already changes the support of missingness, *p*/*n*, specialty, and geography; Challenge 2019 and hip-fracture fill the two remaining empty cells (extreme irregular-lab missingness; orthopedic prediction-time leakage) that those six do not occupy. The open-microdata map is Americas-heavy: high-income national claims (HES, SNDS, HIRA, NDB, CIHI, AIHW, Nordics) will not yield a CSV this sprint, so Latin American line-lists and discharges are the geographic diversity that actually exists, with NFHS-5 as the one simple-registration Asian survey. Credentialed HiRID and MIMIC-IV-ED remain the correct later instruments for European high-frequency ICU and ED triage; they are not substitutes for downloading the eight public tables that already sit behind a URL.
